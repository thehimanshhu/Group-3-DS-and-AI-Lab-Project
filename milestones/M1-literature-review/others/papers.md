Multi-lingual Audio Datasets and Papers

- MLAAD (Müller et al., IJCNN 2024) [multilingual anti-spoofing dataset]
- SpeechFake (ACL 2025) [larger corpus]
- IndicFake + SAFARI-LLM (TMLR 2025) [Indic coverage]
- SEA-Spoof (2025) [South-East Asian languages]
- IndicSynth (Sharma et al., ACL 2025) [Hindi anchor]
- LRLspoof (Borodin et al., 2026) [includes Telugu]
- "Deepfake-audio Detection for Indian Language" (IJERT) [small-scale work]
- ICFD-31k (IJCAI 2026) [Indian Conversational Fraud Dataset]
