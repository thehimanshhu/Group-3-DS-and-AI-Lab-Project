# Kathbath Dataset

Kathbath is a multilingual speech dataset developed by AI4Bharat, containing approximately 1,684 hours of labelled speech across 12 Indian languages, collected from 1,218 contributors across 203 districts in India.

**Source:** [AI4Bharat Kathbath — Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Kathbath)

**Data collection:** Contributors recorded speech by reading sentences sourced from IndicCorp, a collection of text from publicly available websites. The audio was collected through a crowdsourcing process, with associated transcripts and metadata.

**Access:** Hugging Face login and acceptance of the dataset's access conditions are required to access the files.

**Licensing:** The associated IndicSUPERB licensing statement describes the dataset packaging as CC0. The original text is sourced from IndicCorp and is not owned by AI4Bharat.

**Project usage:** We plan to use the Hindi, Tamil and Telugu subsets as genuine human speech for our voice detection model.

Sources: [Kathbath dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Kathbath) and [IndicSUPERB repository](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicSUPERB).
