# 4.5 Regularization and stability

**Setup.** Sweep protocol: 5,000-clip training subset, 3 epochs, full valid set (3,952 clips), seed 42, git commit `51b7f84`. Baseline T-01c is the default XLS-R config on the click-fixed v3 data: **1.341% valid EER**. Numbers come from `results/V-*/run.json` and `epoch_log.csv`; negative Δ is better.

| Run | Change | Train EER | Valid EER | Δ vs T-01c | Best epoch |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| T-01c | default | 1.200% | 1.341% | — | 3 |
| V-01 | dropout 0.0 | 0.200% | 1.265% | −0.076 pp | 3 |
| V-02 | dropout 0.3 | 1.000% | 1.012% | −0.329 pp | 2 |
| V-03 | dropout 0.5 | 0.500% | 0.734% | −0.607 pp | 2 |
| V-04 | weight decay 0.0 | 0.900% | 1.063% | −0.278 pp | 2 |
| V-05 | weight decay 0.1 | 0.600% | 0.911% | −0.430 pp | 2 |
| V-11 | no warmup, no grad clipping | 0.500% | 0.481% | −0.860 pp | 3 |

**Findings.**

- **Dropout improves monotonically: 0.0 → 0.3 → 0.5.** V-03 (0.734%) is the best dropout setting. V-01 has the largest train/valid gap (0.200% train vs 1.265% valid), consistent with overfitting; 0.5 reduces that gap.
- **Weight decay 0.1 is the best tested value, but the default is not a sharp optimum.** V-05 reaches 0.911%. V-04 (weight decay 0.0, 1.063%) also beats the 0.01 default, so the 0.0 vs 0.1 ordering should be treated as noise on one seed.
- **V-11 is the best V run but is a combined change.** It removes both warmup and gradient clipping, reaching 0.481% valid EER. The 3-epoch budget favours no warmup, so the gain cannot be attributed to removing clipping; this needs a full-length, 3-seed ablation.
- **The top three are close.** A paired bootstrap over the 3,952 valid clips (2,000 resamples) gives V-03 0.734% [0.505, 1.139], V-09 0.607% [0.354, 0.836], and V-11 0.481% [0.304, 0.759]; all three exclude the T-01c EER of 1.341%. Their pairwise differences are not resolved: V-11 − V-09 = −0.089 pp, 95% CI [−0.329, +0.152].
- **Caveat.** These are single-seed, 3-epoch runs, and the baseline itself was still improving at epoch 3 (3.09% → 1.90% → 1.34%). Small gaps are not a reliable final ranking.

**Candidates for the final config.** Dropout 0.5, weight decay 0.1, and codec / phone augmentation (4.4); test warmup and clipping at full length. No final decision is made here.
