# Project Title: Deepfake Voice Detection for Indian Languages

With the growth of Generative AI, fake voices and voice-based scams are becoming more common and difficult to identify. Through this project, we aim to develop a system that can detect AI-generated voices and identify potential scams by analysing both audio and spoken content in English, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu. We plan to combine real speech samples with AI-generated voices to create a dataset and use deep learning models to classify audio as real or fake. Additionally, we will analyse the spoken content using NLP to identify suspicious patterns, such as requests for money, OTPs, personal information, or urgent payments. The system will provide a confidence score, highlight suspicious audio segments, and indicate whether the recording may be a scam. Finally, we aim to build a simple web application and  bot to Telegram help users identify suspicious calls and voice messages.
